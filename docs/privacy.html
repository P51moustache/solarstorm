Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

SolarStorm ("the app") shows live space-weather and aurora information. We built it to be private by default. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

What we collect

  • Location (optional). If you grant location permission, the app uses your device's approximate coordinates to calculate the aurora forecast for where you are and to look up local cloud cover and sunrise/sunset times. Your coordinates are sent to weather data providers (Open-Meteo) to retrieve that forecast. We do not store your location on our servers.
  • Saved places & alert settings. These are stored locally on your device.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or share your personal data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics/tracking SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Third-party services

When you request a local forecast, your approximate coordinates are sent to a third-party weather data provider to retrieve cloud cover and sunrise/sunset times. These services receive only what's needed to return data and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Data retention & deletion

Locally stored data (saved places, alert preferences) is removed when you delete the app.

Children

SolarStorm is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change accordingly.
