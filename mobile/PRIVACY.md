# SolarStorm — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-01_

SolarStorm ("the app") shows live space-weather and aurora information. We built it to be
private by default. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

## What we collect

- **Account (optional).** If you create an account, we store your email address and an encrypted
  password through our authentication provider, Supabase, so you can sign in. You can use most of
  the app without an account.
- **Location (optional).** If you grant location permission, the app uses your device's approximate
  coordinates to calculate the aurora forecast for where you are and to look up local cloud cover
  and sunrise/sunset times. Your coordinates are sent to weather data providers (Open‑Meteo) to
  retrieve that forecast. We do not store your location on our servers.
- **Saved places & alert settings.** These are stored locally on your device.

## What we do NOT do

- We do **not** sell or share your personal data.
- We do **not** use third-party advertising or analytics/tracking SDKs.
- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites.

## Data sources

Space-weather and imagery come from public sources: NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center, NASA
(SDO/SOHO), and Open‑Meteo. These services receive only what's needed to return data (e.g. your
coordinates for a local forecast) and are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Data retention & deletion

Locally stored data (saved places, alert preferences) is removed when you delete the app. To delete
your account and associated email, contact us at the address below and we will remove it.

## Children

SolarStorm is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
