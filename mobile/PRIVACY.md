# SolarStorm — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-23. The published copy is `docs/privacy.html`; keep the two in step._

SolarStorm ("the app") shows live space-weather and aurora information. We built it to be
private by default. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

## What we collect

- **Location (optional).** If you grant location permission, the app uses your device's approximate
  coordinates to calculate the aurora forecast for where you are and to look up local cloud cover
  and sunrise/sunset times. Your coordinates are sent to weather data providers (Open‑Meteo) to
  retrieve that forecast. We do not store your location on our servers.
- **Saved places & alert settings.** These are stored locally on your device.
- **Anonymous usage and crash reports.** The app sends anonymous events to PostHog, our analytics
  provider: which screens are opened, when the app is opened or closed, whether onboarding was
  finished, alerts switched on or off and a place saved, plus crash and error reports (error message,
  stack trace, app version, device model and iOS version). Events carry a random ID created on your
  device, not your name, email, location, place names or anything you type. We do not use your IP
  address to estimate where you are, and we do not record your screen. We use this only to see how
  the app is used and to fix crashes.

## What we do NOT do

- We do **not** sell or share your personal data.
- We do **not** use advertising SDKs, and our analytics is never used for advertising.
- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites.

## Third-party services

Space-weather and imagery come from public sources: NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center, NASA
(SDO/SOHO), and Open‑Meteo. These services receive only what's needed to return data (e.g. your
coordinates for a local forecast) and are governed by their own privacy policies. Anonymous usage
and crash reports are processed by PostHog (https://posthog.com/privacy) on our behalf.

## Data retention & deletion

Locally stored data (saved places, alert preferences) is removed when you delete the app. Deleting
the app also removes its random analytics ID, so later reports cannot be linked to earlier ones.

## Children

SolarStorm is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
